# HisaabSaathi: November 2026

## In one paragraph

HisaabSaathi went from 0.6 to 0.9.5 this month.
- Setup is shorter.
- Speech is understood far better.
- The open voices now read entries aloud in Odia, Telugu, Kannada and Tamil.
- Pay is checked against minimum wages in ten states, with the government notice behind each figure.

We also built, but have not yet switched on, the way workers who agree can help the app learn from
their own speech. Our testers use the 0.9 builds.

## What changed in the app

[Download the latest (0.9.5)](https://huggingface.co/AcxelinLabs/hisaabsaathi-model/resolve/main/HisaabSaathi.apk) · Android 10+

- **Understanding** (0.6–0.7.3):
  - many more ways of saying numbers and asking questions, in all six languages, including numbers
    said in English inside Indian sentences and common mishearings;
  - the small on-device model is now Qwen3-0.6B, fine-tuned by us. It labelled all 140 held-out
    test sentences correctly; the previous model got 127.
- **Voices:** answers are read aloud with open Indic Tiny TTS voices in Hindi, Odia, Telugu,
  Kannada and Tamil. An open Odia voice was one of the gaps we named this year.
- **Ledger** (0.6.3–0.7.2):
  - change a saved line, undo a delete, back up and restore;
  - the ledger is grouped by day, with totals;
  - fixes for double saves and stuck listening.
- **Setup** (0.9.2–0.9.4):
  - the language is the worker's choice;
  - a PIN code fills in the state and district, offline. The directory is India Post's: 19,297 PIN
    codes;
  - "Use my location" is optional;
  - the details can come from an Aadhaar QR or be spoken.
- **Welcome** (0.9.5): "Welcome to your HisaabSaathi" with a short sound, in the worker's language.
- **Help improve HisaabSaathi** (0.8.0): an opt-in question, read aloud, with nothing chosen for the
  worker.
- **Removed:** the separate Recorder app. Recording now happens only inside HisaabSaathi, and
  only with consent.

## What we learned

- The app used to pick its language from where the phone was. It now follows the worker's choice,
  everywhere in the app.
- District names had changed in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and other states since India Post's
  lists. The app uses today's names, and offers every possible district when a PIN spans two.
- In Tamil, one Home button cut off its label; it was fixed in 0.9.4. Long labels in Indian
  scripts need room. We now check every screen in all six languages before a release.
- Most minimum-wage figures online come from compliance websites that disagree with each other.
  Only the government notice can be trusted.

## Data and models

- **Minimum wages:** an open dataset for ten states and the central government, each figure with
  its notice and how it was checked
  ([`../../data/minimum-wages/`](../../data/minimum-wages/README.md)).
  - Read from the signed notices: Odisha, Karnataka, Maharashtra (construction), West Bengal, and
    Gujarat's October allowance.
  - Still to check against the notices: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.
- **Model:** the fine-tuned Qwen3-0.6B is on Hugging Face (Apache-2.0), with the comparison against
  Gemma 3 1B and Qwen2.5-0.5B.
- **Learning from workers:** the upload service and nightly job are built and tested. What phones
  send is sealed on the phone, and the job removes identifying numbers. The data stays in Azure in
  India. It is not running yet: it needs the team's Azure setup.
